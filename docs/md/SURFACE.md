FIRST VIEWPORT: A clear large heading and direct web action join a dominant real Today screen and a secondary Calendar screen. The visible controls and actual app interface carry the composition; no invented metrics or endorsements.

FORM: User-pinned product walkthrough from the approved implementation plan; preserve the established app identity. Direct build, no image mockups. Browser-language entry and explicit locale navigation remain ordinary links.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
